# ThayerMahan

> ThayerMahan, Inc. is a U.S. maritime autonomy and ocean-intelligence company headquartered in Groton, Connecticut, founded in 2016 and led by former U.S. Navy Vice Admiral Michael Connor. It operates uncrewed surface and subsurface sensing systems that combine passive acoustic arrays, electronic sensors, edge processing, AI classification and human analysts to deliver persistent undersea surveillance, port and critical-infrastructure security, subsea infrastructure monitoring, passive acoustic marine-mammal monitoring and illegal-fishing (IUU) detection for defense, government and commercial offshore customers.

## Systems

- [Outpost](https://www.thayermahan.com/systems/outpost): Persistent autonomous maritime surveillance; 90+ day unattended endurance with automatic detection, classification and near-real-time reporting.
- [SeaPicket](https://www.thayermahan.com/systems/seapicket): Deployable detection and monitoring barrier system.
- [SeaGuard](https://www.thayermahan.com/systems/seaguard): Non-kinetic UUV defeat system for ports and critical infrastructure.
- [TransparenSea](https://www.thayermahan.com/systems/transparensea): Modern data stack platform — the central repository and processing hub for collected maritime data; delivers contact reports by API, voice or other means.
